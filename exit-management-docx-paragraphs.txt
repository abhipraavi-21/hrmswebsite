What is Exit Management Software?
Exit Management Software is a system that helps HR and admin teams record and track an employee's exit journey, starting from the day a resignation is submitted to the day the exit record is completed. For businesses that still track resignations on paper or in spreadsheets, this process is often slow, inconsistent and hard to track across departments and branches.
Problems with Manual Resignation Tracking
Resignation details are noted in different formats by different people, making records inconsistent.
It is difficult to quickly check who has resigned, from which department, and since when.
Important details like resignation date and last working day get missed or updated late.
Problems Tracking Notice Periods
Without a central record, HR has to manually track each employee's last working day separately.
Cross-checking notice period status across branches and departments takes time and effort.
Problems Managing Employee Exits
Termination cases are handled outside the regular resignation record, creating two separate processes.
Supervisors and HR do not always have visibility into the same exit details.
Problems Maintaining Exit Records
Old resignation and termination records are hard to find when needed for reference.
There is no easy way to search or filter past exit records by department, status or date.
Business Benefits
Altroz HR Exit Management Software brings resignation and termination details into one system. HR teams can record a resignation, track the notice period through the last working day, capture termination details when applicable, and maintain a complete, searchable exit record for every employee. This gives HR teams, admin teams and business owners a clear, organised view of employee exits across the organisation.

Exit Management Workflow
Altroz HR follows a simple, step-by-step workflow to manage every employee exit from start to finish:
1. Employee Submits Resignation
The process begins when an employee decides to resign from the organisation.
2. HR Records Resignation
HR creates a New Resignation record in Altroz HR, capturing the employee, resignation date, branch, department and description.
3. Notice Period Tracked
Once the resignation is recorded, HR tracks the notice period against the recorded resignation date.
4. Last Working Day Managed
HR updates the Last Working Day field as the notice period progresses or is finalised, so the exit date is always current.
5. Termination Details Updated (If Applicable)
For cases involving termination, HR records the Employee Termination details, including supervisor and termination details.
6. Exit Record Completed
The resignation or termination information comes together as a completed Employee Exit Record, which HR can search, filter and print whenever required.
